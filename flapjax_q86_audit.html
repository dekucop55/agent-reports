5. What the chain can and cannot show

Two EOAs that have never received BNB, one of which received a FLAPJAX transfer from the treasury, are equally consistent with:

  • two separate operators holding two fresh wallets, or
  • one operator holding two pre-generated addresses.

The funder test cannot separate those cases here, because the BNB funding it depends on does not exist for either wallet. The count of two operators is the upper bound, not a finding of two operators.
