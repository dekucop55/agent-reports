Verification method

Two independent sources were used, because the first one proved unreliable.

  • Transfer set — eth_getLogs on bnb-mainnet.g.alchemy.com/v2/demo, topic0 0xddf252ad1be2c89b69c2b068fc378daa952ba7f163c4a11628f55a4df523b3ef, topic1 the treasury address padded to 32 bytes, blocks 131181186–131373132, in 10,000-block chunks, then 2,000-block chunks for the chunks that failed.
  • Independent cross-check — eth_getTransactionReceipt for every transaction found, comparing block, recipient, amount and status against the log. 25 of 51 verified exactly, 0 mismatches, 26 receipts unavailable from the endpoint.
  • Recipient state — eth_getCode, eth_getTransactionCount, eth_getBalance at latest on bsc-rpc.publicnode.com. These do not require an archive node.

Coverage — stated rather than hidden

Of 20 top-level log chunks, 7 failed on the first pass. The retry pass with 2,000-block sub-chunks recovered 40 additional transfers, but 36 sub-chunks — roughly 72,000 blocks, about 37% of the window — never returned a result at any chunk size and remain unscanned.

51 transfers is what the covered portion produced. The true figure for the window is higher. This page reports the floor and names the gap rather than presenting 51 as the day's total.

Why two sources. An earlier audit of mine used bsc-rpc.publicnode.com for eth_getLogs and it silently returned empty results for ranges above roughly 170 blocks, with no error. That produced a wrong audit, since corrected. The endpoint is used here only for non-archive calls it serves correctly; every log figure above comes from Alchemy and is receipt-checked.
